Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Drillmates is a small, independent browser game. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and the choices you have. We keep it to the minimum needed to run the game and save your progress.

Who we are

Drillmates is made by an independent developer ("we", "us"). For any privacy question or request, see Contact below.

What we collect

InformationWhenWhy
A random account ID The first time you play online. You start as a guest; no name, email or password is needed. To recognise your guest account and keep your progress.
Your Discord or Google account details: your account ID with that service, email address, display name and profile picture link Only if you choose to connect Discord or Google to keep your progress across devices. To let you sign in and load the same progress on another device.
Game progress: your miner name, money, upgrades, attack levels, quests, pod paint and hat, and play statistics (such as ore mined and creatures defeated) While you play online. To save and restore your game.
Gameplay information: your pod's position, actions and what you type as your miner name While you're connected to a game world. To run the shared world. Other players in your world see your miner name, pod look and rank.
Technical information: your IP address and basic browser details When your browser connects to our servers. Needed to deliver the game and kept briefly in our providers' logs for security and reliability.

We do not use advertising, we do not sell or share your information for marketing, and the game does not include analytics or tracking.

Information stored in your browser

The game uses your browser's local storage (not tracking cookies) to remember your settings (such as volume), your miner name, your chosen pod look, your sign-in session, and your progress when playing offline. You can clear this at any time by clearing your browser's site data. Clearing it signs you out, and an unconnected guest's progress cannot then be recovered.

Who processes your information

We use a few trusted services to run the game:

  • Supabase: sign-in and the database that stores accounts and saves. Data is held in their London, UK region.
  • Discord and Google: only if you choose to sign in with them. Their own privacy policies apply to your use of their services.
  • Our game server host: runs the shared game worlds.
  • GitHub Pages: hosts this website.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, under their own safeguards.

How long we keep it

  • Guest accounts (not connected to Discord or Google) and their progress are deleted automatically after 60 days without play.
  • Connected accounts and their progress are kept while you play, and deleted if you ask us to.
  • Technical logs are kept only as long as our providers need them for security and reliability.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you, correct, export or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it. Contact us and we'll respond within one month. If you're in the UK, you can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Our lawful basis for using your information is to provide the game you've asked to play (and, for technical logs, our legitimate interest in keeping it secure and working).

Children

Drillmates is a friendly game for all ages, but connecting a Discord or Google account is only for people old enough to have one under those services' rules (at least 13). Younger players can play as a guest, which uses no personal details.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. For significant changes we'll let players know in the game.

Contact

For questions or requests, open an issue at github.com/Smiter15/drillmates/issues. Please don't post personal details there: just ask us to get in touch, and we'll reply privately.
